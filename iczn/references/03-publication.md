# Chapter 3 — Criteria of Publication (Articles 7–9)

Source: https://code.iczn.org/criteria-of-publication/ · 4th edition 1999, Articles 8–9 amended 2012 (electronic publication)

This chapter decides whether a document counts as "published" for nomenclatural purposes at all — the gate a new name or nomenclatural act must pass before Chapter 4's availability rules (`references/04-availability.md`) can even apply. Land here to date-stamp a work's publication status, especially for anything issued electronically, on optical disc, or only partially distributed.

**2012 amendment scope:** the electronic-publication amendment to the Code (effective 2012) rewrote Art. 8.1.3, 8.4, 8.5, 8.6, and Art. 9.2–9.12, and also touches Arts. 10, 21 and 78 (see `references/17-commission.md` for Art. 78 registration duties). Where a sub-article below has both a 1999 and a 2012 form, both are given; the 2012 form is the one currently in force for a publication-status determination made today, but the 1999 form still governs works whose publication predates the change and is required to interpret older availability decisions.

## Article 7 — Application
Scopes the whole chapter beyond just new names.

- **7** — The publication criteria of this chapter apply not only to the first publication of a new scientific name, but to publication of *any* nomenclatural act (e.g. designation of a type, First Reviser action) or of information likely to affect nomenclature. [Art. 7](https://code.iczn.org/criteria-of-publication/article-7-application/#art-7)

## Article 8 — What constitutes published work
The affirmative test: a work is "published" only if it meets every criterion here and is not excluded by Art. 9.

- **8** — A work is published within the meaning of the Code only if it satisfies Art. 8's requirements *and* is not caught by an Art. 9 exclusion; failing either prong means no name or act in it is nomenclaturally available. [Art. 8](https://code.iczn.org/criteria-of-publication/article-8-what-constitutes-published-work/#art-8)
- **8.1.1** — The work must be issued to create a public and permanent scientific record (not a private or ephemeral document). [Art. 8.1.1](https://code.iczn.org/criteria-of-publication/article-8-what-constitutes-published-work/#art-8-1-1)
- **8.1.2** — It must be obtainable, on first issue, free of charge or by purchase — not restricted to a closed group. [Art. 8.1.2](https://code.iczn.org/criteria-of-publication/article-8-what-constitutes-published-work/#art-8-1-2)
- **8.1.3 (1999 text)** — It must be produced in an edition of simultaneously obtainable copies made by a method yielding numerous identical, durable copies. [Art. 8.1.3](https://code.iczn.org/criteria-of-publication/article-8-what-constitutes-published-work/#art-8-1-3)
- **8.1.3 (2012 text, current)** — Same edition/simultaneity requirement, but the "durable copies" method now branches: **8.1.3.1** numerous identical durable physical copies (see Art. 8.4 below), **or 8.1.3.2** widely accessible electronic copies with fixed content and layout (the Example names PDF/A, ISO 19005-1, as a qualifying fixed-layout format — a plain, re-flowable web page does not qualify). **Amended 2012.** [Art. 8.1.3](https://code.iczn.org/criteria-of-publication/article-8-what-constitutes-published-work/#art-8-1-3)
- **8.2** — A work can disclaim itself out of publication: if it states it is not issued for public/permanent scientific record or not for nomenclatural purposes, it is not published under the Code even if otherwise distributed (e.g. pre-symposium abstract volumes with a disclaimer). [Art. 8.2](https://code.iczn.org/criteria-of-publication/article-8-what-constitutes-published-work/#art-8-2)
- **8.3** — Individual names or acts *within* an otherwise-published work can be disclaimed and are then unavailable, even though the work itself may still count as published (its other content can still serve as source information, per Art. 8.7.1). [Art. 8.3](https://code.iczn.org/criteria-of-publication/article-8-what-constitutes-published-work/#art-8-3)
- **8.4 (1999 text)** — A work produced before 1986 had to be produced on paper by a then-conventional printing method (letterpress, offset) or by hectograph/mimeograph. [Art. 8.4](https://code.iczn.org/criteria-of-publication/article-8-what-constitutes-published-work/#art-8-4)
- **8.4 (2012 text, current)** — Only paper and optical disc are recognized *physical-copy* formats, subject to Art. 8.1 and not excluded by Art. 9: **8.4.1** paper works must, before 1986 and after 2012, be printed with ink or toner (i.e. the 1986–2012 window is where non-ink/toner paper processes were briefly tolerated under the interim 1985–2000 rule below); **8.4.2** an optical-disc (e.g. CD-ROM) work is published only if issued read-only *after 1985 and before 2013*, and either (8.4.2.1) if issued before 2000, states in the work that it is intended as a permanent record and issued in a simultaneously-obtainable edition, or (8.4.2.2) if issued 2000–2012, names at least five major publicly accessible depository libraries in the work itself. **A CD-ROM/optical-disc work issued before 1986 or in 2013 or later is never published under Art. 8.4.2 — see also Art. 9.3 below, which excludes it outright.** **Amended 2012.** [Art. 8.4](https://code.iczn.org/criteria-of-publication/article-8-what-constitutes-published-work/#art-8-4)
- **8.5 (1999 text)** — A non-conventionally-printed work from 1985–2000 could still count as published if it met the other Art. 8 requirements, stated the author's intent that it be a permanent record, and stated it was issued in a simultaneously-obtainable edition. [Art. 8.5](https://code.iczn.org/criteria-of-publication/article-8-what-constitutes-published-work/#art-8-5)
- **8.5 (2012 text, current)** — Governs electronic-only publication. To be published, an electronically issued/distributed work must: **8.5.1** have been issued after 2011; **8.5.2** state its publication date within the work itself; and **8.5.3** be registered in ZooBank (the Official Register of Zoological Nomenclature, Art. 78.2.4 — see `references/zoobank.md`) with evidence of that registration appearing in the work itself (e.g. the ZooBank registration number, even if only as an embedded PDF hyperlink not visible in normal viewing). Sub-clauses: **8.5.3.1** the ZooBank entry must name a third-party archiving organization (not the publisher) able to permanently preserve the work's content and layout — this need not appear in the work itself; **8.5.3.2** the ZooBank entry must carry an ISBN (for a book) or ISSN (for the journal) — also need not appear in the work; **8.5.3.3** a registration-evidence error does not make the work unavailable provided the work is unambiguously traceable to a ZooBank record created *before* publication (harmless errors: a mistyped digit, a UTC/local-time date mismatch; fatal errors: registering only after the fact, or reissuing a corrected edition under the original, un-reregistered ZooBank number). **Amended 2012 — electronic-only works have no publication route at all before this amendment; a pre-2012 electronic-only "publication" is not available under the Code.** [Art. 8.5](https://code.iczn.org/criteria-of-publication/article-8-what-constitutes-published-work/#art-8-5)
- **8.6 (1999 text)** — A non-paper work produced after 1999 needed a statement, in the work itself, that copies were deposited (in the form published) in at least five named major publicly accessible libraries. [Art. 8.6](https://code.iczn.org/criteria-of-publication/article-8-what-constitutes-published-work/#art-8-6)
- **8.6 (2012 text, current)** — Replaced by a general grant of authority: the Commission may issue Declarations to clarify whether new or unconventional production/distribution/formatting/archiving methods qualify as published under the Code — i.e. the five-library rule is no longer a fixed criterion for new methods; the Commission decides case-by-case. **Amended 2012.** [Art. 8.6](https://code.iczn.org/criteria-of-publication/article-8-what-constitutes-published-work/#art-8-6)
- **8.7** — A work suppressed by the Commission using its plenary power [Art. 81, `references/17-commission.md`] remains published under the Code unless the Commission expressly rules it never was. [Art. 8.7](https://code.iczn.org/criteria-of-publication/article-8-what-constitutes-published-work/#art-8-7)
  - **8.7.1** — Such a suppressed work still counts as a valid *source* of descriptions/illustrations, but no name or nomenclatural act (e.g. a type fixation, or a First Reviser precedence determination under Art. 24.2) can be made available *in* it. [Art. 8.7.1](https://code.iczn.org/criteria-of-publication/article-8-what-constitutes-published-work/#art-8-7-1)
- **8.8 — Permanence of available works.** Once a work is published under the Code, it stays published unless the Commission itself rules otherwise; a later disclaimer or **retraction** of the work (or part of it) by the author, publisher, or anyone else does not undo the availability of the original names/acts it contains. **Added by Declaration 46 (2011/2012); the Glossary term "retraction" was correspondingly defined by the same Declaration — see `references/glossary.md`.** This closes the door on authors or journals trying to "unpublish" an available name by after-the-fact retraction. [Art. 8.8](https://code.iczn.org/criteria-of-publication/article-8-what-constitutes-published-work/#art-8-8)

**Recommendations (all advisory, none mandatory):**
- Rec. 8A — publicize new names/acts widely: register in ZooBank, publish in recognized journals, notify the Zoological Record (2012 wording adds the ZooBank channel; 1999 wording did not).
- Rec. 8B — 1999: prefer paper publication; 2012 (renumbered): a paper edition should be at least 25 copies before distribution.
- Rec. 8C — 1999: keep published copies in publicly accessible libraries; 2012 (renumbered): electronic works should be structured for automated indexing/data extraction with actionable links (e.g. to ZooBank).
- Rec. 8D — 1999: authors/editors/publishers should make a work's published status self-evident, with date and availability stated; 2012 (renumbered): a work's content is immutable once published — corrections belong in a separate erratum, and reprints must be labeled as such with their own date.
- Rec. 8E — 1999: avoid ambiguous near-publications (e.g. undisclaimed pre-symposium abstracts); 2012 (renumbered): keep published copies in publicly accessible libraries.
- Rec. 8F/8G (2012 only) — restates the 1999 8D/8E self-evidence and disclaimer advice under new numbers.
- Rec. 8H (2012 only) — electronic works should be archived with more than one archiving organization holding a permanent or irrevocable license to keep the work accessible.

**Amended:** Art. 8.1.3, 8.4, 8.5, 8.6 and Recommendations 8A–8H — 2012 electronic-publication amendment. Art. 8.8 — Declaration 46 (2011).

**Database relevance:** a `publicationStatus`/`availabilityBasis` model needs, at minimum: medium (paper / optical disc / electronic), issue date, and — for electronic works — a ZooBank registration ID (see `references/zoobank.md`) and evidence-of-registration flag; the record should reject "electronic, pre-2012" as a valid available-publication combination outright under Art. 8.5.

## Article 9 — What does not constitute published work
The exclusion list: even if a work would otherwise pass Art. 8, these categories are never published under the Code.

**1999 list (9.1–9.9), for dating pre-2012 determinations:**
- **9.1** — post-1930 handwriting reproduced in facsimile, by any process. [Art. 9.1](https://code.iczn.org/criteria-of-publication/article-9-what-does-not-constitute-published-work/#art-9-1)
- **9.2** — photographs as such. **9.3** — proof sheets. **9.4** — microfilms. **9.5** — acoustic recordings made by any method. **9.6** — specimen labels. **9.7** — copies obtained on demand of an unpublished work, even if archived in a library. **9.8** — text or illustrations distributed only as electronic signals (e.g. the World Wide Web). **9.9** — abstracts/papers/posters/lecture texts issued primarily to meeting/symposium/congress participants. [Art. 9.2–9.9](https://code.iczn.org/criteria-of-publication/article-9-what-does-not-constitute-published-work/#art-9-2)

**2012 list (current), 9.1 unchanged, 9.2–9.12 renumbered and expanded:**
- **9.1** — unchanged: post-1930 handwritten facsimiles. [Art. 9.1](https://code.iczn.org/criteria-of-publication/article-9-what-does-not-constitute-published-work/#art-9-1)
- **9.2 (new)** — works produced after 1985 by hectographing or mimeographing (these low-fidelity duplication methods, tolerated as "conventional" through 1985 under the old Art. 8.4, are excluded once produced after that date). [Art. 9.2](https://code.iczn.org/criteria-of-publication/article-9-what-does-not-constitute-published-work/#art-9-2)
- **9.3 (new) — optical-disc window.** Works issued on optical disc (e.g. CD-ROM) **before 1986 or after 2012** are not published works. Combined with Art. 8.4.2's affirmative window (issued read-only *after 1985 and before 2013*, i.e. 1986–2012 inclusive, with the accompanying library-deposit or intent statement), the practical rule is: **only an optical-disc work issued in the 1986–2012 window, meeting Art. 8.4.2's statement/deposit conditions, can ever be an available publication; optical discs before that window or from 2013 onward are categorically excluded** — a post-2012 CD-ROM must instead qualify as an electronic work under Art. 8.5 (ZooBank registration) to be published at all. [Art. 9.3](https://code.iczn.org/criteria-of-publication/article-9-what-does-not-constitute-published-work/#art-9-3)
- **9.4–9.8** — photographs, proof sheets, microfilms, acoustic records, specimen labels: same substance as the 1999 9.2–9.6, renumbered. [Art. 9.4–9.8](https://code.iczn.org/criteria-of-publication/article-9-what-does-not-constitute-published-work/#art-9-4)
- **9.9 (new)** — preliminary versions of a work made accessible electronically *in advance of* its formal publication (e.g. an "online early"/in-press version) — see Art. 21.8.3 (`references/05-date.md`) for how this affects the name's effective date once the work is later formally published. [Art. 9.9](https://code.iczn.org/criteria-of-publication/article-9-what-does-not-constitute-published-work/#art-9-9)
- **9.10** — materials issued primarily to meeting/symposium/congress/workshop participants, including abstracts and presentation/poster texts — same substance as 1999 9.9, renumbered and widened to include workshops. [Art. 9.10](https://code.iczn.org/criteria-of-publication/article-9-what-does-not-constitute-published-work/#art-9-10)
- **9.11 (amended)** — text or illustrations distributed only as electronic signals (e.g. via the Internet) are excluded, **except** those that satisfy Art. 8.1 and Art. 8.5's electronic-publication requirements — this is the carve-out that makes a properly ZooBank-registered electronic work publishable, where the 1999 Art. 9.8 had no such exception. [Art. 9.11](https://code.iczn.org/criteria-of-publication/article-9-what-does-not-constitute-published-work/#art-9-11)
- **9.12** — facsimiles or on-demand reproductions of an unpublished work remain unpublished, even from a library/archive copy — same substance as 1999 9.7, renumbered. A print-on-demand reproduction of an originally-unpublished document (e.g. a restricted-circulation thesis) stays unpublished unless a genuine editorial process (repagination, reformatting, new headers) was applied in producing it. [Art. 9.12](https://code.iczn.org/criteria-of-publication/article-9-what-does-not-constitute-published-work/#art-9-12)

**Recommendations:** Rec. 9A (both versions, advisory) — authors should keep new names and nomenclatural acts out of meeting abstracts/posters entirely, and ensure abstract volumes carry an Art. 8.2 disclaimer, to avoid accidental publication.

**Amended:** Art. 9.2–9.12 — 2012 electronic-publication amendment (Art. 9.1 unchanged since 1999).

**Database relevance:** a publication-medium enum should model the optical-disc window as a closed interval (1986–2012 inclusive) rather than an open-ended "CD-ROM allowed" flag, and should treat "electronic, no ZooBank ID" and "online-early/in-press version" as explicitly *unpublished* states rather than nulls.

**Common errors:** assuming any electronic PDF is automatically published because it "looks like" a paper reprint — Art. 9.11's exception only applies if the Art. 8.5 ZooBank-registration conditions are actually met; assuming a CD-ROM issued in 2013+ is treated the same as one from the 1990s — it is not (Art. 9.3).
