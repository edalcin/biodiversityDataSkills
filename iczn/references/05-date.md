# Chapter 5 — Date of Publication (Articles 21–22)

Source: https://code.iczn.org/date-of-publication/ · 4th edition 1999, amended 2012 (electronic publication)
This chapter decides what date attaches to a work, and therefore to every name and nomenclatural act it contains, when the work's own statement of date is silent, incomplete, wrong, or contradicted by a separate/preprint/electronic release. Land here whenever a priority dispute (Chapter 6) turns on which of two names is older, or when cataloguing a name whose source publication carries an ambiguous or corrected date.

## Article 21 — Determination of date
Fixes the date of publication of a work (and of names/acts within it) by a cascade of rules, used only when Article 3 (names published before 1758) does not apply.

- **21.1** — The following provisions (21.2–21.9) govern date determination for all works, subject to Article 3. [Art. 21.1](https://code.iczn.org/date-of-publication/article-21-determination-of-date/#art-21-1)
- **21.2** — A date stated in the work is presumed correct unless there is evidence to the contrary. [Art. 21.2](https://code.iczn.org/date-of-publication/article-21-determination-of-date/#art-21-2)
- **21.3** — If the day is not stated, use the earliest day the work is demonstrably shown to exist as published; failing that evidence:
  - **21.3.1** — month and year given but not day → adopt the **last day of the stated month**. [Art. 21.3.1](https://code.iczn.org/date-of-publication/article-21-determination-of-date/#art-21-3-1)
  - **21.3.2** — only the year given → adopt the **last day of the stated year** (31 December). [Art. 21.3.2](https://code.iczn.org/date-of-publication/article-21-determination-of-date/#art-21-3-2)
- **21.4** — If the stated date is shown to be wrong, substitute the earliest demonstrable date of existence as a published work; if day-level evidence is lacking, fall back to 21.3. [Art. 21.4](https://code.iczn.org/date-of-publication/article-21-determination-of-date/#art-21-4)
- **21.5** — A work issued in parts on different days: each part gets its own separately determined date. [Art. 21.5](https://code.iczn.org/date-of-publication/article-21-determination-of-date/#art-21-5)
- **21.6** — A work whose stated date is a range → dated to the **final day of the range**, unless evidence shows that date is wrong or that the work was issued in parts, in which case 21.3–21.5 apply instead. [Art. 21.6](https://code.iczn.org/date-of-publication/article-21-determination-of-date/#art-21-6)
- **21.7** — No date stated at all: adopt the earliest day the work (or the relevant part) is demonstrably shown to exist as published.
  - **21.7.1** — No day-level evidence → fall back to 21.3. [Art. 21.7.1](https://code.iczn.org/date-of-publication/article-21-determination-of-date/#art-21-7-1)
  - **21.7.2** — Electronic-only works must state a publication date (per Art. 8.5.2), even if only incompletely (per 21.3). [Art. 21.7.2](https://code.iczn.org/date-of-publication/article-21-determination-of-date/#art-21-7-2)
- **21.8** — Advance distribution of separates/preprints:
  - **21.8.1** — Before 2000: distributing separates ahead of the work's stated date **advanced** the date of publication to the separate's distribution date. [Art. 21.8.1](https://code.iczn.org/date-of-publication/article-21-determination-of-date/#art-21-8-1)
  - **21.8.2** — After 1999: advance issue of separates does **not** advance the date. By contrast, paper preprints unambiguously bearing their own date are themselves published works dated from their date of issue, provided they meet Art. 8's publication criteria and are not excluded by Art. 9. [Art. 21.8.2](https://code.iczn.org/date-of-publication/article-21-determination-of-date/#art-21-8-2)
  - **21.8.3** — Preliminary online versions posted ahead of a work's final published version do **not** advance the date — such advance access is not "publication" (Art. 9.9). [Art. 21.8.3](https://code.iczn.org/date-of-publication/article-21-determination-of-date/#art-21-8-3)
- **21.9** — A name or act appearing in a work issued **both** on paper and electronically takes its date from whichever edition first satisfies Art. 8's publication criteria (and is not excluded by Art. 9) — i.e. dated to first qualifying release, print or electronic, whichever is earlier. [Art. 21.9](https://code.iczn.org/date-of-publication/article-21-determination-of-date/#art-21-9)

**Recommendations:** Rec. 21A (do not release a work other than on its stated date; do not distribute advance separates until publication is certain) · Rec. 21B (all data affecting availability, incl. type fixation, should appear in the same work on the same day) · Rec. 21C (editors/publishers should state exact day of publication for each part) · Rec. 21D (librarians should preserve date-bearing covers/pages) · Rec. 21E (separates should carry a full citation of the original work, incl. date; preprints should clearly self-date) · Rec. 21F (authors aware of an incorrect/incomplete stated date should publish a correction). All advisory only.

**Amended:** 2012 electronic-publication amendment restructured 21.6–21.9 and added 21.7.2, 21.8.3, and the whole of 21.9 (paper+electronic dual issue) to accommodate electronic works; see also `references/03-publication.md` for the Art. 8/9/10 electronic-publication gateway these date rules depend on.

**Database relevance:** Store the *adopted* publication date (a resolvable calendar date per 21.3–21.9), not just a transcribed year string — a record needs to distinguish "year stated in work" from "date adopted under the Code" when they diverge (21.4, 22A.2.2). For part-issued serials, date at the part level (21.5), not the volume level.

**Common errors:** Treating a volume's spine year as the date of every name in it, ignoring that individual parts/fascicles may carry distinct dates (21.5) or that separates before 2000 could predate the work itself (21.8.1).

## Article 22 — Citation of date
Governs how a date is written next to a name, and confirms the date is a citation practice, not part of the name itself.

- **22.1** — When a date is cited with a name, it follows the author's name (see Art. 51 for full author-citation rules). [Art. 22](https://code.iczn.org/date-of-publication/article-22-citation-of-date/#art-22)

**Recommendations (Rec. 22A, advisory only):**
- **22A.1** — Cite date (and authorship) at least once per work dealing with a taxon; especially important for homonyms and for species-group names not in their original combination.
- **22A.2** — Method: no more than a comma between author and date (22A.2.1); if the actual date of publication differs from the work's imprint date, cite the **actual** date (22A.2.2); to show both, cite the actual date first, then the imprint date bracketed/quoted for information (22A.2.3) — e.g. `Ctenotus alacer Storr, 1970 ("1969")` for a work published 1970 but imprint-dated 1969.
- **22A.3** — When an original species-group date is cited under a changed genus (parenthesised authorship, Art. 51.3), the date sits inside the same parentheses as the original author — e.g. `Limax ater Linnaeus, 1758` cited as `Arion ater (Linnaeus, 1758)`.

**Database relevance:** The date belongs on the **authorship string** (a citation field), never concatenated into or treated as part of the taxon-name string itself — Art. 22 makes this explicit. Model `scientificNameAuthorship`/`namePublishedInYear` as fields distinct from `scientificName`, and keep a separate flag for "imprint date differs from actual date" (22A.2.2) so both can be retained without corrupting the primary date field used for priority comparisons (Chapter 6).
